I talked to a contact from the municipal financing division at PNC bank today
to educate myself on bond financing and see how it may work for the SID. 

High level is we could expect to incur somewhere between $200-300k in expenses.
Most of these are flat costs that are not proportional to our financed amount.
Doing the math, this means a bond issuance could add between 33-50% on top of
our capital improvement needs for a cluster septic or new WWTP. That seems very
cost-prohibitive. 

From start to finish, it could take 3-4 months to issue the bond. Parties
involved would likely include bond council, municipal advisor, underwriter,
paying agent, rating agency, and others. 

Rates may be about 6.5% for a 10yr or 7.4% for a 20yr. Could get better rates
if we serialized the bonds (paying off bonds as we go).

Not much institutional demand for offerings under $1 mm. That could push our
rate up even more. 

My contact did say that a bank loan may be more practical for our situation. He
is from Chicago and may not know what is allowable in Nebraska. A bank loan
could be easier to execute, have less fees, and be more flexible for payoff
options. 

Is a loan an option for an SID? I could try to meet with some local banks and
see if they would be willing to finance us and what the costs/rates may be.
